🥽 Moti Holo-Room
Checking WebXR…

Your dashboard, floating around you. Every card from the control panel — lights, Spotify, notes, PC stats — becomes a holographic window you can grab, move and pinch shut. Don't want one? Throw it in the trash bin to dismiss it.

HOW TO USE
  • Grab — pinch (thumb + index) near a window's title bar and move your hand to drag it.
  • Click — poke a button on a window with your fingertip, like pressing a real button.
  • Throw away — while grabbing a window, carry it to the glowing trash bin and release the pinch to dismiss it.
  • Restore — poke "↺ Restore All" on the dock panel that follows you to bring dismissed windows back.
  • No hand tracking? Use your controller's trigger to grab and click instead.
Note: browsers don't expose real camera pixels to web pages, so Moti can't visually recognize an actual trash can. Instead, on first entry it asks you to tap a real spot on your floor (via WebXR surface detection) to anchor the virtual trash bin there.
